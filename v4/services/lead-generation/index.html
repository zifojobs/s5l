LinkedIn Visibility and Outreach System.

Most AEC companies use LinkedIn as a project gallery. They post finished-project photos, company announcements, and generic messages, but they rarely address what buyers care about or create real conversations with decision-makers.

S5L combines customer-focused videos, executive content, target-account research, strategic engagement, personalized outreach, email follow-up, and conversation tracking. We do not post and wait; we use content to build familiarity, outreach to start conversations, and follow-up to turn interest into qualified inquiries.

What every video has to do.

Three jobs, and a film for each.

  1. 01

    Pain points

    Buyers don’t hire contractors. They hire people who understand their problems. Our videos put you on camera speaking directly to your industry’s pain points. When a prospect checks your LinkedIn, they already know you’re the one who can fix it.

  2. 02

    Customer testimonials

    Your best sales pitch isn’t yours. It’s your customer’s. The people who staked their own success on your work are the most convincing voice you have, which is why we create testimonial videos that let them do the talking and build confidence in you before you ever get in the room.

  3. 03

    Scroll stopper

    The industry has a reputation for being blue collar, nail-and-hammer, all function and no flair. We push against that. Every video we produce opens with a hook designed to stop the scroll and make your audience actually watch.

Platinum Construction had no sales function.

Pure word of mouth and referrals. In roughly three months we produced twelve meaningful conversations with national brands — McDonald’s, Tim Hortons, Subway and A&W — and placement on a vendor list they had never been on.

Twelve conversations is activity. A procurement gate that stays open after the campaign ends is a change to what they are eligible to bid on.
