Talent Acquisition Through Video

Recruitment videos should be created for one clear purpose: showing the right candidates why they should choose your company. Through company-culture stories, recruiter interviews, behind-the-scenes content, and day-in-the-life videos, candidates understand your workplace, hiring process, and expectations before they apply.

The recruitment series.

Eight formats. Each one answers a question a candidate is already asking.

  1. 01

    Recruiter interview

    Explain the hiring steps, timing, what candidates should prepare, and what the company looks for.

  2. 02

    Day in the life

    Follow an executive, project manager, estimator, foreperson, technician, apprentice, or office employee through a real workday.

  3. 03

    Employee story

    Let current employees explain why they joined, what they have learned, and why they stayed.

  4. 04

    Company culture story

    Show how people communicate, solve problems, stay safe, grow, and support one another.

  5. 05

    Role-specific video

    Explain the work, expectations, team, tools, schedule, and growth path for one open position.

  6. 06

    Candidate FAQ series

    Answer common questions about experience, certifications, travel, training, benefits, and the hiring process.

  7. 07

    Leadership message

    Let a founder or executive explain what the company is building and who will succeed there.

  8. 08

    Onboarding preview

    Show what happens after someone accepts the job so the first days feel clear and welcoming.

One distinction worth making.

A golf event may show that the company is social, but many construction companies hold golf events. It does not automatically explain why a skilled candidate should join. Recruitment content has to show the work, the people, the expectations, the growth, and the employee experience that is actually yours.
