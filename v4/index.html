What AEC gets wrong on LinkedIn.

Not a lack of effort. Six habits, and every one of them is fixable.

  • 01

    They treat LinkedIn like Facebook

    Broadcast to a company page, then hope.

  • 02

    Post and ghost

    No engagement before or after publishing, when the algorithm requires both.

  • 03

    The company page as the main lever

    The personal page is what actually carries reach and trust.

  • 04

    Before-and-after as the entire strategy

    It proves you finished a building. Every bidder can prove that.

  • 05

    No funnel

    Nothing at the top, nothing in the middle, everything asking for the meeting.

  • 06

    A quiet belief that it cannot generate leads
